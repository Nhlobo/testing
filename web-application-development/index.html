How We Work

Our Development Process

A clear, collaborative four-stage approach that keeps you informed at every step and delivers working software on schedule.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Deep-dive workshops with your team to map business goals, user journeys, technical constraints, and success metrics. We document everything before we propose solutions.

  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes, high-fidelity UI designs, and interactive prototypes. Architecture decisions and tech stack selection. Client sign-off before development begins.

  3. 03

    Develop

    Two-week sprint cycles with regular demos. Automated testing, code reviews, and CI/CD pipelines. You see working software throughout — no big-bang reveals.

  4. 04

    Launch & Grow

    Staged production deployment, monitoring setup, and knowledge transfer. Ongoing performance optimisation, security updates, and new feature delivery.
