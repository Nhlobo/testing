Terms of Service

These terms apply to all project work delivered by North Lab Studio.

1. Engagement Scope

Each project begins with a written scope that defines deliverables, timeline, and revision rounds.

2. Payment Terms

Projects are billed in two stages: 50% upfront to reserve production time, and 50% before final delivery.

Work starts once the deposit is received. Final files, deployment handoff, and transfer of access are completed after final payment.

3. Cancellation Policy

If a client cancels before production starts, any paid deposit can be refunded minus administrative costs.

If cancellation occurs after project work has started, a cancellation penalty applies based on completed work and time already allocated.

4. Delivery Conditions

Delivery dates depend on timely feedback and content provided by the client. Delays in approvals may shift milestones.

Requested changes outside the signed scope are treated as additional work and quoted separately.

5. Support and Warranty

All projects include a 30-day support window for bug fixes related to the agreed scope.
