The Challenge

A growing South African municipality faced massive queues at its service centres — residents waiting hours for bill queries, permit applications, and service fault logging. Some residents were taking half-days off work to resolve queries that should take minutes.

The existing municipal website was outdated, mobile-unfriendly and had no transactional capability whatsoever. The municipality's call centre was completely overwhelmed. The solution needed to handle sensitive citizen data in full compliance with POPIA, while being accessible to residents across the digital divide — including those on basic smartphones and limited data.

Project Goals

01

Create a POPIA-compliant citizen portal for service requests and account management

02

Enable online bill payments integrated with the municipality's billing system

03

Reduce service centre foot traffic by at least 50% within 12 months

04

Build an accessible, data-light experience for residents across the digital divide

Our Solution

We built a React frontend deployed on Vercel with a Node.js API connecting to the municipality's PostgreSQL database. The portal handles utility bill payments, fault reporting (potholes, streetlights, water leaks), permit status checks, and general service requests — all in a clean, accessible interface.

An admin portal lets municipal staff manage cases, update statuses and trigger SMS notifications to citizens. Every status change automatically sends an SMS to the resident who logged the request — reducing follow-up calls dramatically.

The site was built to WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards and achieved a Lighthouse score of 96. It passed an independent POPIA compliance audit — data minimisation, encryption at rest and in transit, and granular consent management are baked in throughout the architecture.
