Event Catering Commerce & Fulfillment System

A quote-to-delivery platform for catering teams managing complex events and menu logistics.

Overview

The system manages the full catering lifecycle from inquiry and quotation to production planning and delivery execution. It serves growing catering operators needing tighter commercial and kitchen coordination. It matters because margin and service quality depend on operational precision.

Problem

Manual quotations, fragmented menu planning, and disconnected execution workflows were causing delays, errors, and margin leakage.

Solution

We implemented a unified commerce and fulfillment platform with configurable menus, automated quoting, and production scheduling intelligence.

Key Features

  • Configurable menu engine with dietary logic
  • Automated quote generation and approval workflows
  • Production scheduling dashboard for kitchen teams
  • Delivery coordination with client notifications
  • Revenue and event profitability analytics

Design & User Experience

The platform uses a structured workflow UI that guides teams from sales to operations, reducing handoff errors and improving speed of execution.

Technology

HTML5Tailwind CSSJavaScriptPayment Gateway APICloud Database

Impact / Value

The solution reduced quotation turnaround time, improved order accuracy, and enabled teams to scale event volume with greater control.
