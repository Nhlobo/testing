Event Catering Commerce Platform

A digital ordering and operations platform for corporate and private catering teams.

Overview

This solution is a Web initiative designed for the Small Business segment, focused on measurable business performance and long-term scalability.

Problem

Catering teams were losing time to manual quotes, menu back-and-forth, and fragmented event coordination.

Solution

Delivered a platform combining menu configuration, quote automation, and production scheduling in one interface.

Features

  • Dynamic menus with dietary preference logic
  • Automated quote generation and approvals
  • Kitchen production timeline dashboards
  • Delivery coordination and client notifications

Design & UX

We designed the interface for high confidence and low cognitive load, using clear hierarchy, operationally relevant dashboards, and responsive flows for desktop and mobile stakeholders.

Technology

HTML5Tailwind CSSJavaScriptPayment Gateway

Impact / Results

The platform improved operational efficiency, reduced manual coordination overhead, and provided leadership with better decision intelligence—unlocking scalable growth and stronger service quality.
