Automated Car Care Booking Suite

A booking and membership platform for high-throughput car wash businesses.

Overview

This solution is a Apps initiative designed for the Small Business segment, focused on measurable business performance and long-term scalability.

Problem

Walk-in only operations caused long waits, uneven demand, and limited visibility into repeat customer behavior.

Solution

Created a web-first booking suite with slot management, package subscriptions, and real-time queue updates.

Features

  • Time-slot booking with capacity control
  • Membership and package management
  • Queue status tracking for customers
  • Revenue and repeat-visit analytics

Design & UX

We designed the interface for high confidence and low cognitive load, using clear hierarchy, operationally relevant dashboards, and responsive flows for desktop and mobile stakeholders.

Technology

HTML5SCSSVanilla JSStripe Integration

Impact / Results

The platform improved operational efficiency, reduced manual coordination overhead, and provided leadership with better decision intelligence—unlocking scalable growth and stronger service quality.
