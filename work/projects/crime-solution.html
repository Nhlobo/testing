Crime Solution App

A community safety app concept with incident reporting, location sharing, and neighbourhood alerts.

Overview

This solution is a Mobile App initiative designed for the Government segment, focused on measurable business performance and long-term scalability.

Problem

Public safety agencies relied on siloed reporting and delayed intelligence sharing, slowing response to crime patterns.

Solution

Built a secure intelligence platform for incident aggregation, hotspot analysis, and inter-agency collaboration.

Features

  • Incident intake and case lifecycle management
  • Geospatial hotspot mapping and trend analysis
  • Cross-unit collaboration and escalation workflows
  • Executive reporting for strategic planning

Design & UX

We designed the interface for high confidence and low cognitive load, using clear hierarchy, operationally relevant dashboards, and responsive flows for desktop and mobile stakeholders.

Technology

HTML5JavaScriptSecure APIsGIS Integration

Impact / Results

The platform improved operational efficiency, reduced manual coordination overhead, and provided leadership with better decision intelligence—unlocking scalable growth and stronger service quality.
