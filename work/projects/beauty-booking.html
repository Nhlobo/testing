On-Demand Beauty Services App

Connected customers with vetted beauty professionals for at-home appointments.

Overview

This solution is a Apps initiative designed for the Small Business segment, focused on measurable business performance and long-term scalability.

Problem

Beauty providers needed a reliable way to manage availability and clients while customers wanted trustworthy, easy booking.

Solution

Developed an appointment marketplace app with professional profiles, geolocation matching, and automated reminders.

Features

  • Provider profiles and trust verification
  • Location-aware service matching
  • In-app payments and cancellation policy logic
  • Reminder workflows that reduce no-shows

Design & UX

We designed the interface for high confidence and low cognitive load, using clear hierarchy, operationally relevant dashboards, and responsive flows for desktop and mobile stakeholders.

Technology

HTML5JavaScriptFirebaseMaps API

Impact / Results

The platform improved operational efficiency, reduced manual coordination overhead, and provided leadership with better decision intelligence—unlocking scalable growth and stronger service quality.
