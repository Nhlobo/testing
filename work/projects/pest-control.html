Field Service Operations Management System

A service operations platform that digitizes bookings, dispatch, and reporting for local service businesses.

Overview

This system centralizes customer intake, technician dispatch, and post-service documentation for pest-control operations. It serves small service teams that need reliable execution with limited admin capacity. The platform matters because it reduces missed appointments and creates a dependable service standard.

Problem

Teams relied on phone bookings and manual schedules, leading to routing inefficiencies, inconsistent communication, and limited visibility into technician performance.

Solution

We built a field-service operations system that unifies booking workflows, route allocation, client notifications, and digital job reports in one operational environment.

Key Features

  • SLA-driven booking and scheduling console
  • Geo-aware dispatch board for field allocation
  • Automated SMS and email service notifications
  • Digital treatment records with customer history
  • Operations dashboard for team utilization and SLA tracking

Design & User Experience

The UI prioritizes speed and clarity for office coordinators and mobile technicians, with responsive layouts and action-first controls that reduce operational friction.

Technology

HTML5JavaScriptNode.js APIPostgreSQLCloud Hosting

Impact / Value

The system improved route efficiency, reduced no-shows, and increased service capacity without increasing administrative headcount.
