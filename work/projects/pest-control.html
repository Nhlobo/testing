Field Service Operations Platform

Digitized pest control operations from service requests to technician dispatch and reporting.

Overview

This solution is a Systems initiative designed for the Small Business segment, focused on measurable business performance and long-term scalability.

Problem

Manual booking and dispatch processes created missed appointments, delayed treatments, and inconsistent service quality.

Solution

Implemented a centralized operations system connecting bookings, route planning, and technician reporting in one flow.

Features

  • Online booking and SLA-based scheduling
  • Geo-aware dispatch board for field teams
  • Automated SMS/email service notifications
  • Digital treatment reports and client history

Design & UX

We designed the interface for high confidence and low cognitive load, using clear hierarchy, operationally relevant dashboards, and responsive flows for desktop and mobile stakeholders.

Technology

HTML5JavaScriptNode APIPostgreSQL

Impact / Results

The platform improved operational efficiency, reduced manual coordination overhead, and provided leadership with better decision intelligence—unlocking scalable growth and stronger service quality.
