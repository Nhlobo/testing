A construction company website built to present services, completed work, and quote requests.

Overview

This solution is a Corporate Website initiative designed for the Growing Business segment, focused on measurable business performance and long-term scalability.

Problem

Construction firms struggled with fragmented project data, resulting in delays, rework, and communication bottlenecks.

Solution

Developed a command center aggregating schedules, milestones, procurement, and site updates across teams.

Features

  • Milestone and dependency tracking
  • Site progress photo logs and approvals
  • Subcontractor coordination workflows
  • Executive dashboards for delivery risk

Design & UX

We designed the interface for high confidence and low cognitive load, using clear hierarchy, operationally relevant dashboards, and responsive flows for desktop and mobile stakeholders.

Technology

HTML5JavaScriptREST APIsData Visualization

Impact / Results

The platform improved operational efficiency, reduced manual coordination overhead, and provided leadership with better decision intelligence—unlocking scalable growth and stronger service quality.
