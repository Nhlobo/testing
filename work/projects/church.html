Community Engagement Church Platform

A modern church website driving engagement, events participation, and digital giving.

Overview

This solution is a Web initiative designed for the Small Business segment, focused on measurable business performance and long-term scalability.

Problem

Disconnected communication channels made it difficult to keep members informed and involved in community activities.

Solution

Created a centralized engagement platform for events, livestreams, giving, and member communication.

Features

  • Sermon library with topic and speaker filters
  • Events calendar and volunteer sign-ups
  • Secure online giving workflows
  • Segmented notifications for ministries and groups

Design & UX

We designed the interface for high confidence and low cognitive load, using clear hierarchy, operationally relevant dashboards, and responsive flows for desktop and mobile stakeholders.

Technology

HTML5CSS3JavaScriptDonation API

Impact / Results

The platform improved operational efficiency, reduced manual coordination overhead, and provided leadership with better decision intelligence—unlocking scalable growth and stronger service quality.
