Climate Intelligence Decision Dashboard

Enterprise-grade weather analytics supporting operations, logistics, and risk planning.

Overview

This solution is a SaaS initiative designed for the Enterprise segment, focused on measurable business performance and long-term scalability.

Problem

Organizations lacked unified weather intelligence to proactively plan operations and mitigate climate-related disruptions.

Solution

Engineered a multi-source dashboard delivering forecast models, alerts, and location-specific risk scoring.

Features

  • Multi-source weather data fusion
  • Custom risk indices by operation type
  • Automated threshold alerts and escalations
  • Scenario planning dashboards for leadership

Design & UX

We designed the interface for high confidence and low cognitive load, using clear hierarchy, operationally relevant dashboards, and responsive flows for desktop and mobile stakeholders.
