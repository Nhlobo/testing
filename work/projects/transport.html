School Transport Coordination & Safety System

A transport operations platform that improves route reliability and parent communication.

Overview

This system digitizes daily transport operations with route planning, rider verification, and real-time alerts. It serves school transport providers and institutions managing student logistics at scale. The platform matters because predictable and safe transport is mission-critical for education services.

Problem

Manual tracking methods created route inconsistencies, delayed communication, and limited accountability for daily transport performance.

Solution

We built a coordination and safety system with route optimization, pickup verification, and instant parent notification workflows.

Key Features

  • Route optimization and stop-level planning dashboard
  • Student check-in and check-out verification logs
  • Real-time delay and arrival notifications
  • Driver performance and compliance reporting
  • Incident tracking and escalation workflows

Design & User Experience

The UX is operationally focused, with high-contrast data views and clear alert states that help coordinators and drivers respond quickly under real-world conditions.

Technology

HTML5JavaScriptRealtime DatabaseNotification ServiceMapping API

Impact / Value

The platform improved route punctuality, strengthened safety accountability, and reduced inbound support calls from parents.
