School Transport Management System

A route and rider management system improving safety, punctuality, and parent visibility.

Overview

This solution is a Systems initiative designed for the Small Business segment, focused on measurable business performance and long-term scalability.

Problem

Transport coordinators tracked riders manually, making daily route changes and parent communication error-prone.

Solution

Built a transport control platform with route assignment, pickup verification, and live notification workflows.

Features

  • Route planning with stop-level optimization
  • Student check-in/check-out verification
  • Instant delay and arrival notifications
  • Driver performance and route reporting

Design & UX

We designed the interface for high confidence and low cognitive load, using clear hierarchy, operationally relevant dashboards, and responsive flows for desktop and mobile stakeholders.

Technology

HTML5JavaScriptRealtime DatabaseNotification Service

Impact / Results

The platform improved operational efficiency, reduced manual coordination overhead, and provided leadership with better decision intelligence—unlocking scalable growth and stronger service quality.
