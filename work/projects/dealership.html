Automotive Commerce & Rental Platform

A full-funnel platform for vehicle listings, financing leads, and rental operations.

Overview

This solution is a Apps initiative designed for the Growing Business segment, focused on measurable business performance and long-term scalability.

Problem

Dealerships managed sales and rentals in disconnected systems, creating slow lead response and poor inventory visibility.

Solution

Built an automotive platform combining listing management, financing workflows, and rental scheduling.

Features

  • Inventory management with rich media listings
  • Lead qualification and financing inquiry flows
  • Rental booking calendar and pricing logic
  • Sales dashboard with conversion pipeline visibility

Design & UX

We designed the interface for high confidence and low cognitive load, using clear hierarchy, operationally relevant dashboards, and responsive flows for desktop and mobile stakeholders.

Technology

HTML5JavaScriptCRM IntegrationAnalytics Stack

Impact / Results

The platform improved operational efficiency, reduced manual coordination overhead, and provided leadership with better decision intelligence—unlocking scalable growth and stronger service quality.
