The Challenge

A Johannesburg taxi association with 200+ vehicles had no digital system for route management, driver scheduling or vehicle maintenance tracking. Dispatchers used notebooks and WhatsApp to coordinate the entire fleet. Revenue leakage from untracked trips was estimated at R800,000 per year.

Driver disputes over route assignments and earnings were frequent, damaging morale and retention. Management had zero visibility into real-time fleet performance, route profitability or vehicle maintenance status. The association needed a complete digital overhaul — but it had to work for drivers on basic Android smartphones with limited data.

Project Goals

01

Build a driver-facing mobile app for trip tracking and earnings visibility

02

03

Automate route assignment and fare calculation to eliminate disputes

04

Track vehicle maintenance schedules to reduce costly breakdowns

Our Solution

We built a React Native app for drivers (iOS and Android) with GPS trip tracking, earnings dashboard and push notifications. The app was optimised for low-end Android devices — lightweight, data-efficient, and usable on basic 3G connections.

A Node.js REST API handles all business logic, with MongoDB storing trip, driver and vehicle data. A web-based dispatcher dashboard built in React provides a real-time map view of all active vehicles, with drag-and-drop route assignment and instant push notification to the driver.

Automated route assignment uses a custom algorithm based on zone availability, driver ratings and current vehicle locations. Vehicle maintenance tracking sends automatic alerts to the operations manager when service intervals are approaching, reducing unexpected breakdowns by over 60%.
