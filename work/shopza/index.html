The Challenge

ShopZa was a 15-branch Gauteng retail chain relying entirely on in-store sales. When lockdown hit, they lost 60% of their revenue overnight with no online fallback. Their existing attempts at online selling — Facebook posts and WhatsApp orders — were unscalable and error-prone, with orders getting lost and no inventory visibility.

They needed a full e-commerce platform capable of handling inventory synchronisation across 15 branches, SA payment processing, delivery logistics management and customer accounts — all integrated with their existing POS system.

Project Goals

01

Launch a mobile-first e-commerce platform in under 12 weeks

02

Integrate PayFast for secure SA payment processing including EFT and QR code

03

Synchronise inventory across all 15 physical branches in real-time

04

Enable same-day delivery booking and tracking across Gauteng

Our Solution

We built a custom React storefront with server-side rendering for SEO, backed by a Node.js API layer connecting to PostgreSQL. PayFast integration handles card, EFT and QR code payments — the full range that SA consumers expect. A real-time inventory sync engine uses PostgreSQL triggers to update stock levels across all 15 branches the moment a sale is made.

An admin dashboard lets branch managers view live sales, adjust stock levels, manage product listings, and process refunds without needing developer access. The platform was built mobile-first throughout, with images served as WebP and lazy-loaded, achieving a Lighthouse mobile score of 94.

The delivery booking module integrates with third-party couriers for same-day Gauteng delivery, with customers receiving WhatsApp notifications at each stage of the delivery process.
