The Challenge

A private clinic group with 12 locations had no unified patient management system. Each clinic used different software — some were still entirely paper-based. Patients couldn't book appointments online. No-show rates had climbed to 35%, wasting clinical time and revenue.

Referral management between clinics was handled via phone and fax. A patient seeing a specialist at a different branch might have their records arrive late, out of sequence, or not at all. The group was losing revenue from inefficiency and losing patients to competitors with better digital experiences. They needed a complete, integrated solution — with full POPIA compliance for sensitive medical data.

Project Goals

01

Unified patient records accessible securely across all 12 clinics

02

Online appointment booking with real-time slot availability across all clinics

03

Automated appointment reminders to significantly reduce no-show rates

04

Secure payment processing with Stripe for consultation fee collection

Our Solution

We built a React web app backed by Node.js and MongoDB. Patient records are stored with full POPIA compliance — encrypted at rest, access-logged with a complete audit trail, and with patient consent management built into every data collection flow.

The booking engine manages real-time slot availability across all 12 clinics simultaneously. Automated WhatsApp and SMS reminders at 48 hours and 2 hours before appointments reduced no-shows from 35% to 14%. Stripe processes consultation fee payments with card and EFT, and invoices are automatically generated and emailed.

A dedicated doctor portal lets clinicians update records, view full patient history and issue digital referrals to other clinics in the network — eliminating the fax machine entirely. Receptionists have a clean appointment management view per clinic.
