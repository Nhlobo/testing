The Challenge

Heritage Hotels was a boutique hotel group with 6 properties across the Western Cape. They were paying 18–22% commission to OTA platforms (Booking.com, Expedia) on every booking — a crippling cost that ate into already-thin hospitality margins. Their own website had no booking capability whatsoever.

Guest loyalty wasn't being rewarded or tracked — repeat guests got no recognition. Communication between guests and properties was fragmented across WhatsApp and phone calls with no central record of preferences, complaints or history. They needed a direct booking platform and a modern guest experience system — but it had to look beautiful enough to compete with major hotel brands.

Project Goals

01

Build a direct booking platform to reduce OTA commission dependency

02

Create a loyalty programme rewarding repeat guests with redeemable points

03

Centralise guest communications and preferences across all properties

04

Integrate Yoco for both online and in-property terminal payments

Our Solution

We built a React-based booking platform backed by Node.js and PostgreSQL, hosted on AWS. The booking engine manages real-time room availability across all 6 properties with dynamic pricing — rates adjust based on season, occupancy and advance booking window.

Yoco integration handles both online payments and in-property terminal payments from a single system, simplifying reconciliation. The loyalty programme tracks guest stays and awards redeemable points — guests can redeem for room upgrades, dining credits and experiences.

A guest communications portal gives front desk staff a unified view of all guest messages, preferences and history — so when a returning guest arrives, the front desk already knows they prefer ground floor rooms and allergies. An automated pre-arrival and post-stay email/WhatsApp sequence improved review volumes significantly, contributing to the 4.8★ rating.
