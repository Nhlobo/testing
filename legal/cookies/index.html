1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, tablet, or mobile phone) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and to provide information to the owners of the site.

2. How We Use Cookies

Mapengo Innovations uses cookies on this website for the following purposes:

  • Necessary cookies — These are essential for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as page navigation and access to secure areas. The website cannot function properly without these cookies.
  • Analytics cookies — We use these to understand how visitors interact with our website, including which pages are most visited, how long visitors stay, and where they come from. This helps us improve the website experience. These cookies collect information anonymously.
  • Preference cookies — These allow the website to remember choices you make (such as your cookie consent preferences) to provide a more personalised experience.
  • Marketing cookies — We may use these to track visitors across websites with the purpose of displaying relevant advertising. These cookies are only set with your explicit consent.

4. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

When you first visit our website, a cookie consent banner will be displayed. You can choose to accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or customise your preferences.

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

  • See what cookies are set and delete them individually
  • Block third-party cookies
  • Block all cookies from specific sites
  • Block all cookies from being set
  • Delete all cookies when you close your browser

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of the website. Necessary cookies cannot be opted out of if you wish to use the site.

We are committed to compliance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa. Our use of cookies and the personal data they may collect is governed by our Privacy Policy. Where cookies collect personal information, we do so only with your consent (except for strictly necessary cookies).
