POPIA Compliance Statement

Last updated: January 2025

What is POPIA?

The Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA) is South Africa's comprehensive data protection legislation, which came into full effect on 1 July 2021. It regulates how organisations collect, store, use, share and protect the personal information of individuals. POPIA is broadly similar in purpose to the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and is designed to promote a culture of privacy and data protection in South Africa.

Our Commitment

At Mapengo Innovations, we take our POPIA obligations seriously. We are committed to processing personal information responsibly, lawfully and transparently. Protecting the privacy and personal information of our clients, website visitors and any individuals whose data we may process is a fundamental part of how we operate. We continuously review and improve our data protection practices.

How We Comply

  • Appointed an Information Officer responsible for overseeing POPIA compliance
  • Maintain a record of all personal information processed by us
  • Implemented appropriate security safeguards to protect personal information
  • Provide data subjects with access to their personal information upon request
  • Only collect information that is necessary for the specified purpose
  • Ensure proper consent is obtained where required before processing
  • Delete or anonymise information that is no longer needed

Data Subject Rights

Under POPIA, individuals (data subjects) whose personal information we process have the following rights:

  • Right of Access: You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Correction: You have the right to request that we correct any inaccurate or incomplete personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to Deletion: You have the right to request that we delete your personal information where it is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected.
  • Right to Objection: You have the right to object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Complain to Information Regulator: If you believe your rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa.

Data Retention

We retain personal information for the duration of our service relationship with you and for a period of 5 years after the relationship ends, in order to comply with legal and regulatory obligations. After this retention period, personal information is securely deleted or anonymised.

Security Measures

  • Encrypted data storage
  • Secure HTTPS connections for all web communications
  • Role-based access controls limiting data access to authorised personnel only
  • Regular security audits and vulnerability assessments
  • Staff training on data protection principles and obligations

Third Party Processors

We work with reputable third-party service providers who process personal information on our behalf. We ensure that all such processors are bound by POPIA-compliant data processing agreements that require them to maintain appropriate security standards. Our primary processors include:

  • Web and cloud hosting providers for data storage and delivery
  • Analytics tools for understanding website usage patterns

We do not share personal information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.
