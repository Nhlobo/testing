Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

1. Introduction

Mapengo Innovations ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it and what rights you have regarding your personal information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Name and contact details
  • Company name and role
  • Project information
  • Website usage data (cookies and analytics)
  • Communication history with us

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Respond to project enquiries
  • Provide services you've engaged us for
  • Send relevant updates about your project
  • Improve our services and website
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Service providers (hosting, analytics) bound by data processing agreements
  • Legal requirements, such as compliance with a court order or applicable law
  • With your explicit consent

5. Cookies

We use cookies to improve your experience on our website, including analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site and functional cookies to remember your preferences. You can control cookie settings through your browser. For more detail, please refer to our Cookie Policy.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and meet legal obligations — typically 5 years after project completion for records. After this period, information is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights Under POPIA

  • Right to access your personal information
  • Right to correct inaccurate information
  • Right to delete your information (right to erasure)
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to complain to the Information Regulator

8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information, including encrypted data storage, HTTPS connections, role-based access controls, and regular security audits. Despite these measures, no internet transmission is completely secure, and we encourage you to take care when sharing sensitive information.
