## The Business Problem

Most South African businesses outgrow their off-the-shelf software before they realise it. Spreadsheets break under scale. Generic SaaS products don't fit your processes. The result: manual workarounds, duplicated data, and staff spending hours on tasks a system should handle in seconds.

## Our Solution Approach

We design and build custom business systems from the ground up — or integrate and automate your existing tools — so your organisation runs like a well-oiled machine. Every workflow, every report, every integration is tailored to how your business actually operates.

### What We Build

- **Custom CRM systems** — Track leads, clients, and relationships without paying per-seat for generic tools
- **ERP & inventory management** — Stock, procurement, orders, and fulfilment in one unified system
- **HR & payroll workflows** — Leave management, onboarding checklists, and compliance tracking
- **Approval & document workflows** — Digital sign-off, version control, audit trails
- **Reporting dashboards** — Real-time business intelligence your leadership can act on

### Integrations We Support

We connect your custom system to the tools you already use:
- **Accounting** — Xero, Sage, QuickBooks SA
- **Payments** — PayFast, Yoco, Peach Payments, Ozow
- **Communication** — WhatsApp Business API, Twilio SMS, email
- **Government APIs** — SARS eFiling integrations, Home Affairs verification where available
- **Logistics** — The Courier Guy, Aramex, Fastway API connectivity

### Our Integration Approach

- RESTful and GraphQL API architecture
- Webhook-driven real-time event processing
- ETL pipelines for data migration and transformation
- Role-based access control (RBAC) and audit logging
