## Break Down the Silos

Disconnected systems cost businesses time, money, and accuracy. We integrate your existing tools — CRM, ERP, accounting software, and more — into a unified, efficient operation.

### What We Connect

- **CRM systems** — Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho
- **Accounting** — Xero, Sage, QuickBooks
- **Communication** — Slack, WhatsApp Business API, email
- **Payment systems** — All major SA payment gateways
- **Government APIs** — SARS, Home Affairs integrations where available

### Our Integration Approach

- RESTful and GraphQL APIs
- Webhook-driven event systems
- ETL pipelines for data transformation
- Real-time and batch processing options
