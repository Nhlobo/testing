## The Business Problem

Your customers are on their phones. But a website that isn't purpose-built for mobile creates friction — slow load times, awkward navigation, and checkout flows designed for desktop. You're losing customers every day to a better mobile experience from your competitors.

## Our Solution Approach

We build native-quality mobile applications that South African users love — designed for variable network conditions, data-conscious usage, and the diverse device landscape across the country. From simple consumer apps to complex enterprise platforms, we deliver mobile solutions that actually get used.

### What We Build

- **Consumer-facing apps** — Loyalty programmes, ordering, booking, and service delivery apps
- **Business operations apps** — Field service management, delivery tracking, inspection tools
- **Enterprise mobility** — Secure internal tools for your workforce, on any device
- **Hybrid web-to-app** — Upgrade your existing web presence to a full native app experience

### Key Features

- **Cross-platform** — Single codebase for iOS and Android, reducing cost and maintenance
- **Offline-first** — Continues working during load shedding and in poor-connectivity areas
- **SA payment integration** — PayFast, Yoco, Peach Payments, SnapScan built in
- **Push notifications** — Re-engage users with targeted, permission-based messaging
- **App Store ready** — We handle submission, review, and ongoing update management

### Our Technology Stack

- **React Native** — Shared codebase, near-native performance
- **Flutter** — Google's UI toolkit for beautiful, natively compiled apps
- **Supabase / Firebase** — Real-time data sync and authentication
- **Expo** — Faster development cycle with OTA updates
