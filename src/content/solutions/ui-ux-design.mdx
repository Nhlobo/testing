## The Business Problem

Most South African businesses invest in development but neglect design — and wonder why their digital products have high bounce rates, poor conversions, and frustrated users. Poorly designed interfaces cost real money: abandoned carts, failed onboarding, and support tickets that should never have existed.

## Our Solution Approach

Great design is not decoration. It is strategy made visible. We create interfaces grounded in user research and conversion psychology — beautiful enough to impress, intuitive enough that users never need to think about what to do next.

### Our Design Process

1. **Discover** — User interviews, competitor analysis, business goal mapping
2. **Architect** — Information architecture, user flows, content hierarchy
3. **Wireframe** — Low-fidelity sketches reviewed and iterated with your team
4. **Design** — High-fidelity, pixel-perfect screens in Figma
5. **Prototype** — Interactive prototypes for stakeholder sign-off and usability testing
6. **Handoff** — Developer-ready specs, assets, and a living design system

### Key Features & Deliverables

- **Brand-aligned design system** — Colours, typography, spacing tokens, and components
- **Responsive designs** — Desktop, tablet, and mobile at every breakpoint
- **Interactive Figma prototypes** — Click-through flows for stakeholder review
- **Accessibility compliant** — WCAG 2.1 AA standards, usable by all South Africans
- **Conversion-optimised** — CTAs, forms, and flows designed to drive action

### What We Design

- Marketing websites and landing pages
- Mobile apps (iOS and Android)
- Internal business tools and dashboards
- E-commerce checkout and product experiences
- Government citizen-facing portals
