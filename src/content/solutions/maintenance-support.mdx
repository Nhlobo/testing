## We Keep Your Systems Running

Technology requires ongoing care. Our maintenance plans ensure your digital products stay secure, performant, and up to date — so you can focus on your business.

### What's Included

- **Uptime monitoring** — 24/7 alerts if your site goes down
- **Security patches** — Regular updates to dependencies and CMS
- **Performance reports** — Monthly reports on speed, traffic, and errors
- **Content updates** — Text, image, and minor feature updates
- **Backup management** — Regular, tested backups

### Support Tiers

- **Basic** — Monthly updates and monitoring
- **Standard** — Weekly updates, priority support
- **Premium** — Daily checks, dedicated support channel, SLA guaranteed
