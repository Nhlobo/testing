## The Challenge

Juta Daco Construction needed a professional corporate website to present their services, showcase completed projects, and make it straightforward for potential clients to request quotes.

## The Solution

We built a structured, professional corporate website highlighting their construction services, portfolio of completed work, and a streamlined quote-request process.

### Key Features

- Service catalogue with construction specialisations
- Completed projects gallery
- Quote request and contact forms
- Mobile-responsive layout
- Professional brand presentation

## The Results

The website established Juta Daco's credibility online, making it easy for prospective clients to understand their capabilities and reach out for quotes — supporting business development without requiring manual referrals.
