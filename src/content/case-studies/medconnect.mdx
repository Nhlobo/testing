## The Challenge

HealthBridge Africa wanted to bring medical consultations to patients in underserved, rural communities where doctor access is limited. The app needed to work in low-bandwidth environments and on low-end Android devices.

## The Solution

We built MedConnect using React Native for cross-platform compatibility, with Firebase for real-time messaging and offline support. The Node.js backend handled appointment scheduling, medical records, and secure video consultations.

### Key Features

- Video consultations with < 50kbps bandwidth support
- Offline symptom checker
- Prescription and referral generation
- Integration with clinic management systems
- POPIA-compliant data handling

## The Results

MedConnect now serves over 5,000 patients across 12 provinces, with an average consultation initiation time of under 3 minutes. The team at HealthBridge Africa has expanded their reach significantly without proportional cost increase.
