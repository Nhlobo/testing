## The Challenge

RetailEdge SA needed to replace their outdated WooCommerce store with a high-performance platform capable of handling thousands of concurrent shoppers — especially during peak retail periods like Black Friday.

## The Solution

We built a custom React-based e-commerce platform with a Node.js API backend and PostgreSQL database. The architecture was designed for horizontal scalability on AWS, with Redis caching for product listings and CDN delivery of all static assets.

### Key Features

- Real-time inventory management
- PayFast and Yoco payment integration
- Advanced search with Elasticsearch
- Mobile-first, PWA capabilities
- Admin dashboard with analytics

## The Results

Within 6 months of launch, the platform was processing over R2 million in monthly transactions. Load time dropped from 4.2 seconds to 0.8 seconds — a 5x improvement — directly contributing to the 340% revenue increase.
