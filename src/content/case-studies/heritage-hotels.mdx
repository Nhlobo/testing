## The Challenge

Heritage Hotels had an outdated website that failed to convey the premium experience of their properties. The site was slow, not mobile-friendly, and driving bookings through expensive OTA platforms.

## The Solution

We redesigned and rebuilt the Heritage Hotels website with an emphasis on immersive visual storytelling, fast load times, and a seamless booking experience that encourages direct reservations.

### Key Features

- Immersive full-screen photography and video
- Direct booking engine integration
- Virtual property tours
- SEO-optimised property and room pages
- Mobile-first responsive design

## The Results

Direct bookings increased by 180% within 3 months of launch, significantly reducing OTA commission costs. The site achieved a 98/100 PageSpeed score and bounce rate dropped by 45%.
