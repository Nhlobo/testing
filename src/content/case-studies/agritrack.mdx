## The Challenge

FarmFresh Technologies needed a comprehensive platform to help South African farmers track their crops, livestock, and supply chain — from planting to market. The system needed to work on mobile devices even in areas with poor connectivity.

## The Solution

We built AgriTrack with a React frontend and Django REST backend on Azure, with a mobile-first design and robust offline capabilities. Integration with weather APIs and market price feeds provides real-time insights.

### Key Features

- Crop lifecycle tracking with growth stage monitoring
- Livestock management and health records
- Supply chain tracking from farm to market
- Weather integration and alerts
- Market price feeds for 50+ commodities
- Offline-capable mobile interface

## The Results

AgriTrack is now managing over 120 farms across South Africa. Farmers report an average 28% yield increase through better planning and monitoring, and 35% waste reduction through supply chain optimisation.
