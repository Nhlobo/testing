## The Challenge

FinServ Solutions needed a platform to help South African SMEs navigate the complex SARS tax compliance landscape — reducing filing errors and the time business owners spend on tax administration.

## The Solution

We built TaxEase with Next.js and a serverless AWS Lambda backend, with PostgreSQL for data persistence. The platform integrates directly with SARS eFiling APIs and automates VAT calculations, provisional tax reminders, and EMP submissions.

### Key Features

- SARS eFiling API integration
- Automated VAT calculation and submission
- Provisional tax reminders and calculations
- Document vault for tax records
- Multi-entity support for business groups

## The Results

Over 500 SMEs now use TaxEase to manage their tax compliance. Average filing time dropped by 90%, and the error rate is consistently below 0.1% — virtually eliminating SARS penalties for clients.
