## The Challenge

Community members needed a simple, reliable way to report incidents, share real-time location alerts, and coordinate neighbourhood safety — without relying on slow official channels.

## The Solution

We designed and built a cross-platform mobile app using Flutter and Firebase that allows users to report crimes, trigger alerts, and view nearby incidents on a live map.

### Key Features

- Incident reporting with photo attachment
- Real-time location sharing and neighbourhood alerts
- Firebase push notifications for nearby incidents
- Offline-capable report drafting
- Anonymous reporting mode for sensitive cases

## The Results

The app delivers a fast, intuitive safety experience across Android and iOS from a single codebase. Community members can submit a report in under 60 seconds and receive alerts for incidents within a configurable radius.
