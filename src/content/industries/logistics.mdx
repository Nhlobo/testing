## Logistics Is Won and Lost on Visibility

In logistics, what you can't see costs you money. Delayed shipments, disputed deliveries, inefficient routes, and stockroom chaos are not operational inevitabilities — they are information problems. The right digital systems transform reactive logistics operations into proactive, data-driven businesses.

South Africa's geography, infrastructure, and the dominance of road freight make technology even more critical here than in markets with reliable rail or established carrier networks.

## Our Logistics Solutions

### Fleet Tracking & Management
Real-time GPS tracking of every vehicle in your fleet, with geofence alerts, trip history, driver behaviour monitoring, and automated maintenance scheduling. Reduce fuel theft, improve driver accountability, and give clients accurate ETAs.

### Digital Proof of Delivery (ePOD)
Replace paper POD books with a mobile app that captures photos, GPS coordinates, and electronic signatures at point of delivery. Dispute resolution time drops from days to minutes. Invoicing becomes immediate.

### Route Optimisation
Intelligent route planning that considers traffic, load constraints, time windows, and delivery priorities. Reduce fuel costs by 15–25% and increase daily deliveries per driver.

### Warehouse Management System (WMS)
From goods-in to despatch, manage your warehouse with barcode scanning, real-time stock levels, pick-and-pack workflows, and cycle counting. Eliminate mis-picks and reduce the time to locate stock.

### Client Integration & APIs
Many enterprise shippers require EDI or API connectivity before awarding contracts. We build the integration layer that makes your business enterprise-ready — connecting your operations to your clients' supply chain systems seamlessly.

### Customer Tracking Portal
Give your clients the Amazon-style experience they expect: a branded tracking portal where they can follow their shipment from collection to delivery in real time.

## South African Logistics Context

We understand what makes SA logistics unique:
- **Load shedding** — Offline-capable mobile apps that sync when connectivity returns
- **Rural last-mile** — Solutions that work on USSD and basic smartphones for remote delivery points
- **Informal settlements** — Flexible addressing and landmark-based delivery confirmation
- **Cash-on-delivery** — Integrated payment collection at the point of delivery
