## The Challenge Facing SA's Taxi Industry

The minibus taxi industry is one of South Africa's largest employers and the primary mode of transport for millions of commuters. Yet despite its economic importance, most taxi operations still run on paper ledgers, handshakes, and mobile money transfers.

This creates tension between drivers and owners, makes it impossible to grow, and exposes businesses to fraud.

## Our Taxi Management Solutions

### Fleet & Revenue Dashboard
Track every trip, every rand, every day. Know exactly how much each vehicle and driver earns without waiting for end-of-day cash drops.

### Vehicle Maintenance System
Never get surprised by a breakdown again. Schedule services, track parts, and set reminders so vehicles stay on the road.

### GPS Route Tracking
Real-time visibility on where every taxi is — improving safety, reducing unauthorized trips, and giving passengers live arrival times.

### Driver Management
Performance scores, incentive tracking, shift management, and incident reporting — all in one system.

### Association Compliance
Generate reports for taxi association meetings. Manage permits, levies, and route allocations digitally.
