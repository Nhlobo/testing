## Why SMEs Struggle to Scale

South Africa's 2.5 million SMEs create the majority of private-sector jobs. But most hit a growth ceiling not because of lack of opportunity — but because of operational chaos.

When the owner is the system, growth stalls. We build the systems that let you grow beyond yourself.

## Our SME Automation Suite

### Quote-to-Invoice Automation
Generate professional quotes in 60 seconds. Convert to invoices with one click. Send payment requests via WhatsApp. Track what's paid and what's overdue — automatically.

### CRM & Pipeline Management
Track every customer interaction, deal stage, and follow-up in one place. Never let a lead go cold because someone forgot to call back.

### Workflow Automation
Build approval chains, task assignments, and escalation rules that work while you sleep. Reduce human error. Enforce consistency.

### Business Intelligence
Know your numbers: best customers, best margins, most profitable services, slowest-paying clients. Make decisions based on data, not gut.

### Accounting Integration
Connect seamlessly with Xero, Sage One, or QuickBooks. Your financial data stays accurate without double-entry.
