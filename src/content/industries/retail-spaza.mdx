## Why Retail & Spaza Shops Need Digital Tools

South Africa's informal and semi-formal retail sector is the backbone of township and rural economies. Millions of families depend on spaza shops, tuck shops, and small supermarkets for daily essentials. Yet most of these businesses operate without any digital systems.

The result: stockouts, untracked theft, no sales data, and missed opportunities.

## Our Retail Solutions

### Lightweight Point-of-Sale (POS)
A simple, affordable POS designed for South African retail — works on a tablet or phone, accepts cash and card, and generates daily reports automatically.

### Stock Management
Know exactly what's in your storeroom. Get alerts when stock runs low. See which products drive the most profit.

### WhatsApp Integration
Sales summaries, low-stock alerts, and supplier orders — all sent directly to the owner's WhatsApp. No app download required.

### Supplier Management
Track orders, deliveries, and credit terms with all your suppliers in one place.
