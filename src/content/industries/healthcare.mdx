## Healthcare's Digital Transformation Challenge

South African healthcare faces immense pressure: rising demand, staff shortages, and infrastructure gaps. Digital systems don't replace clinicians — they eliminate the administrative burden that takes time away from patients.

Every hour a nurse spends on paperwork is an hour not spent on patient care.

## Our Healthcare Solutions

### Electronic Patient Records
POPIA-compliant digital patient files accessible across your practice. No more lost folders, illegible handwriting, or filing backlogs.

### Appointment Management
Online booking, SMS/WhatsApp reminders, and automated follow-ups reduce no-shows by up to 60% — recovering significant revenue.

### Medical Aid Billing
Submit claims electronically, track rejections, and manage disputes — reducing billing errors and accelerating reimbursements.

### Chronic Disease Management
Recall systems for chronic patients (diabetes, hypertension, HIV/TB) ensure no patient falls out of care. Reduce complications. Improve outcomes.

### Telehealth Integration
Consult with patients remotely via secure video. Issue digital prescriptions. Expand your reach without expanding your premises.
