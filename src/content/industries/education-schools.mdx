## Modernising South African Education

Education is the most powerful lever for transformation in South Africa — but most schools, especially in historically underserved communities, are still running on paper, WhatsApp groups, and manual Excel spreadsheets.

We build practical, affordable digital systems that give teachers more time to teach and give parents real visibility into their children's education.

## Our Education Solutions

### School Administration System
Manage learner records, staff, timetables, and reports from a single dashboard. Generate CAPS-aligned reports with one click.

### Parent Communication Portal
Daily updates, report cards, event notifications, and fee statements — sent directly to parents via the channel they prefer: email, SMS, or WhatsApp.

### Fee Management
Track fees, send automated reminders, accept EFT and PayFast payments, and generate financial reports for the school governing body.

### Digital Attendance
Teachers mark attendance on a phone or tablet. Absenteeism alerts go to parents automatically.

### Learning Management System
For secondary schools and colleges: upload lessons, assignments, and assessments. Students learn at their own pace, even on mobile data.
