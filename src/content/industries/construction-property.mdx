## Why Construction Needs Better Systems

South Africa's construction and property sector is one of the largest employers and GDP contributors. Yet most projects run over budget and over schedule — not because of poor workmanship, but because of poor information flow.

When the site manager doesn't know the budget, when the contractor doesn't get the latest drawings, when compliance docs are in someone's bakkie — that's a systems problem.

## Our Construction & Property Solutions

### Project Management Platform
Track every project, every phase, every task. Assign responsibilities, set deadlines, and get daily progress updates — from head office or on site.

### Subcontractor Management
Manage your entire supply chain digitally. Issue orders, track delivery, approve invoices, and build a performance record for each contractor.

### Budget & Cost Tracking
Know your cost position in real time. Compare actual spend against budget. Get alerts before you overshoot.

### Compliance Management
Store CIDB certificates, OHS files, insurance docs, and inspection reports in a centralised, searchable digital vault.

### Property Portfolio Management
For developers and landlords: manage tenants, leases, maintenance requests, and rental collections from one dashboard.
