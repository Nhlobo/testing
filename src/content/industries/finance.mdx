## Finance's Digital Imperative

South Africa's financial sector is at an inflection point. Challenger banks, mobile money platforms, and embedded finance solutions are disrupting established players. Meanwhile, traditional institutions face mounting regulatory pressure from the FSCA and increasing customer expectations shaped by global neobanks.

The cost of digital inaction in finance is measured in clients lost, regulators frustrated, and competitors gaining ground.
