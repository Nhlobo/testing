Here is a fact that should alarm you: 53% of mobile users abandon a website that takes longer than 3 seconds to load. In South Africa, where mobile data is expensive and connections can be inconsistent, your users are even less forgiving.

## The Real Cost of a Slow Website

Let us be specific about what you are losing:

- **Every 1-second delay** reduces conversions by 7%
- **A 3-second load time** vs 1-second loses you 32% of users before they even see your homepage
- **Google penalises slow sites** — your rankings drop, reducing organic traffic
- **Returning visitors abandon faster** — they remember the bad experience

For a business doing R100,000/month in online revenue, a poor-performing site could be costing you R20,000-R30,000/month.

## Why SA Websites Are Slow

The most common culprits we find when auditing SA business websites:

### 1. Unoptimised Images
A single hero image at 5MB will kill your page speed. Images should be:
- Compressed (use WebP format)
- Sized correctly (do not serve a 2000px image in a 400px container)
- Lazy loaded (only load images when they enter the viewport)

### 2. Too Many Plugins (WordPress)
Every plugin adds JavaScript, CSS, and database queries. A typical WordPress site has 15-20 plugins, many of which are unnecessary. Audit yours ruthlessly.

### 3. No Caching
If your server rebuilds every page on every visit, you are doing unnecessary work. Implement:
- Browser caching (tells users' browsers to remember your assets)
- Server-side caching (serve pre-built pages)
- CDN (serve assets from servers close to your users)

### 4. Hosting on Cheap Shared Servers
R50/month hosting is false economy. Shared servers are overloaded, slow, and unreliable. The revenue you lose from slow performance far exceeds the cost of better hosting.

## How to Measure Your Speed

1. **Google PageSpeed Insights** (free) — Tests your site and gives you a score out of 100
2. **GTmetrix** (free tier) — Detailed waterfall analysis
3. **WebPageTest.org** — Test from SA server locations

Aim for:
- PageSpeed score: 90+
- Time to First Contentful Paint: < 1.5s
- Total Load Time: < 3s

## The Fix

### Quick Wins (Do Today)
1. Compress all images (use squoosh.app or TinyPNG)
2. Enable gzip compression on your server
3. Minimise CSS and JavaScript files

### Medium Term (This Month)
1. Implement a CDN (Cloudflare has a free tier)
2. Audit and remove unnecessary plugins
3. Upgrade hosting if on shared servers

### Long Term (Consider)
1. Rebuild on a modern framework (Astro, Next.js) — these generate static HTML that loads in milliseconds
2. Implement Progressive Web App features
3. Optimise your critical rendering path
