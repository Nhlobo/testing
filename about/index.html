How we work

Our 5-step project process

A structured approach that keeps projects on time, in scope, and free of nasty surprises.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    We start by listening. Understanding your business goals, your customers, your existing systems, and your constraints. A thorough brief prevents expensive rework later.

    • Stakeholder interviews
    • Requirements document
    • Technical feasibility
  2. 02

    Design

    Wireframes first, visuals second. We map user flows and screen layouts before touching colours so that every design decision is rooted in how people actually use the product.

    • Wireframes & user flows
    • UI design in Figma
    • Client sign-off before build
  3. 03

    Build

    Clean, documented code using the right tools for your project. We maintain a staging environment so you can review real progress — not just screenshots — throughout the build.

    • Iterative sprints
    • Staging environment access
    • Progress updates every week
  4. 04

    Test

    Cross-browser, cross-device, and load testing on South African network conditions before anything goes live. We break it in testing so your users don't.

    • Functional QA testing
    • Performance & load tests
    • Accessibility audit
  5. 05

    Launch & Support

    Go-live on your schedule. Post-launch, we monitor, fix bugs, and provide ongoing support — because a website or app isn't a one-time project, it's a living asset.

    • Managed deployment
    • 30-day bug warranty
    • Ongoing retainer available
