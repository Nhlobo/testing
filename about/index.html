Our Story

Mapengo Innovations was founded in 2022 with a single, clear purpose: to build digital products that actually work for South African businesses. Not adapted from foreign templates, not one-size-fits-all — built for South Africa.

We saw how businesses across the country were being failed by software designed for overseas markets — software that ignored load-shedding realities, didn't support SA payment gateways like PayFast and Yoco, and was priced beyond the reach of growing SMEs.

So we built differently. We design for limited data. We build for offline-first resilience. We integrate SA payment infrastructure. And we price for the real South African market — from spaza shop to enterprise.
