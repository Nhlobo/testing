Our Story

How Mapengo Started

Mapengo Innovations began as a small independent build team in Johannesburg, working with businesses that needed straightforward digital support and reliable execution.

Many local teams had websites or systems that looked good but did not help with daily operations. We set out to build tools that are easier to manage, easier to maintain, and aligned with business goals.

Today we work with startups, SMEs, and growing companies across South Africa. Each project starts with scope clarity, followed by iterative delivery, testing, and support after launch.
