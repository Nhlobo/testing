Novice Nhlovo Mathebula

Software Engineer  ·  Full Stack Developer  ·  Founder & CEO

Novice is the Founder & CEO of Mapengo Innovations and the engineer behind every product the company delivers. He started in front-end development and has since advanced into full-stack engineering — building complete digital products end-to-end: from UI and APIs through to databases, deployment, and ongoing improvement after launch.

A graduate of the ALX Software Engineering Program (Foundation of Computer Science & Software Engineering, graduated September 2024), Novice brings rigorous technical training and a builder-first mindset to every project. His goal: make high-quality digital solutions genuinely accessible to South African businesses.

Background & Mission

Built on Genuine Engineering

Mapengo Innovations exists because there's a clear gap between the quality of digital products South African businesses deserve and what they typically have access to. Novice founded the company to close that gap — with engineering that's clean, maintainable, and designed to scale.

Builder-first

Novice cares about outcomes: software that loads fast, feels intuitive, and holds up in production — not demos that look good in a presentation but break under real-world conditions.

Full-stack ownership

From UI through APIs, databases, and deployment: Novice handles the complete picture. That means fewer handoff gaps, tighter consistency, and a product that works as a cohesive whole.

Honest delivery

Mapengo Innovations doesn't operate hosting infrastructure — we deploy to trusted platforms like GitHub Pages and Render, and hand over a product you own and can grow.
