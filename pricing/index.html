Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Are these prices fixed or estimates?

The prices shown are starting guides. Once we understand your specific requirements during discovery, we provide a fixed-price quote with full line-item breakdown. There are no surprise invoices.

How does milestone-based billing work?

We split projects into clear milestones — typically Discovery, Design, Development, and Launch. You pay each milestone only after you've reviewed and approved that phase of work.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. For projects above R 15,000 we offer structured payment plans spread across the project timeline. For larger enterprise projects, monthly retainer arrangements are also available.

What if my project doesn't fit a tier?

Every project is different. If your needs sit between tiers or require unique features, contact us for a custom quote. We scope exactly what you need — nothing more, nothing less.

Is hosting included in the price?

Hosting is not included in the project price, but we recommend and configure hosting for you. South African hosting typically costs R 150–R 600 per month depending on traffic and requirements.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance?

Yes. We offer monthly maintenance retainers starting from R 1,500/month, covering updates, security patches, backups, and minor content changes. Enterprise SLA packages are also available.
