How Payment Works

Simple, Fair Payment Terms

Our payment structure protects both you and us — clear milestones, no surprises.

50% Upfront

A deposit of 50% of the agreed project value is due before any work begins. This covers discovery, planning, and initial design or development work.

50% Before Delivery

The remaining 50% is due before final delivery or deployment. We will not publish or hand over the completed product until the full balance is settled.

Live After Full Payment

Your project goes live only after the full project fee has been received. For larger projects, a milestone-based schedule can be agreed in writing before work starts.

Cancellation Policy

If you need to cancel a project after work has started, you remain liable for all work completed to that date, calculated against the agreed project fee. Any deposits paid are non-refundable once work has begun. Third-party costs already committed on your behalf (such as licences or domain registrations) are also payable. We will provide all completed deliverables on receipt of the outstanding balance.

Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Are these prices fixed or can they change?

The prices shown are starting prices ("from"). The final quote depends on your specific requirements, number of features, integrations needed, and design complexity. We always provide a detailed, fixed quote before starting work so there are no surprises.

Do you require a deposit?

Yes. A 50% deposit is required before any work begins. The remaining 50% is due before final delivery or deployment. Your project goes live only once the full project fee is received. For larger or phased projects, a milestone-based payment schedule can be agreed in writing before work starts.

Do your prices include hosting?

Hosting is not included in the listed prices but can be arranged as an add-on. We can advise on the best hosting solution for your project and budget, from shared hosting to dedicated cloud infrastructure.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. We build with growth in mind. You can always start with a Basic plan and upgrade to add more features, pages, or functionality as your business grows.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept EFT bank transfers and selected mobile payment methods. All payments are in South African Rand (ZAR). Receipts and invoices are issued for every transaction.

Do you offer maintenance after delivery?

Yes. We offer ongoing maintenance and support packages to keep your site or app updated, secure, and running smoothly. Ask about our monthly maintenance plans when requesting a quote.
