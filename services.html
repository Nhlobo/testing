Common Questions

Frequently asked questions.

How long does a typical project take?

Project timelines vary based on scope. A standard marketing website typically takes 4–6 weeks. A custom web application or mobile app ranges from 8–16 weeks. We'll give you a precise timeline during discovery, and we stick to it.

How is pricing structured?

We work on a fixed-scope, fixed-price model for most projects — no surprises. Larger engagements may use a retainer model. We're transparent about pricing from day one and don't charge for scope that wasn't agreed upon.

How many revisions are included?

Our design process includes two structured revision rounds per milestone. We've found this produces better outcomes than unlimited revisions — it keeps decisions focused and projects moving forward. Additional revisions can be added as needed.

Do you work with startups or only established businesses?

We work with both. What matters to us is ambition and commitment to quality. We've helped seed-stage startups build their first product, and we've helped established companies transform legacy systems into modern platforms.

What happens after launch?

We offer ongoing support and maintenance packages for all clients. We also offer performance optimisation retainers for continuous improvement after launch. You won't be left on your own when the project is done.

Ready to start building?

Tell us about your project. We'll respond within 24 hours and provide a clear, honest assessment of how we can help.
