The Process

How Collaboration Works

We keep our collaboration process transparent, efficient, and focused on delivering real value.

1. Discovery & Scope

Step 1

We start with a focused discovery conversation to understand your goals, constraints, and expectations. Together we define the project scope, key deliverables, and success criteria — ensuring both parties are fully aligned before any work begins.

2. Proposal & Timeline

Step 2

We prepare a clear, itemised proposal outlining the work, milestones, timeline, and agreed terms. No vague retainers or hidden costs — you know exactly what you're getting and when.

3. Active Collaboration

Step 3

Work progresses through agreed milestones with regular check-ins, status updates, and shared visibility into progress. We communicate proactively — you're never left wondering where things stand.

4. Delivery & Handover

Step 4

On delivery, we provide full documentation, source assets, and a structured handover. We're available for a post-launch support period and happy to discuss ongoing maintenance or future collaboration.
